# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Overview
An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built with Power BI to explore employee attrition and workforce metrics. The dashboard helps users examine how attrition varies by education field, gender, job role, age group, salary slab, and department.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Track key workforce KPIs.
- Analyze employee attrition count and attrition rate.
- Compare attrition across education fields and job roles.
- Explore patterns by age group, gender, salary slab, and department.
- Use interactive slicers to investigate different employee segments.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills
- **Power BI Desktop** — report design and interactive visualizations
- **Power Query** — data preparation and transformation
- **DAX** — measures and KPI calculations
- **Data visualization** — KPI cards, bar charts, donut charts, tables, and salary-slab comparisons

## 📌 Dashboard KPIs
- Employee Count
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Salary
- Average Years of Service

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals
| Visual | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Attrition by Education Field | Compares attrition counts across education backgrounds |
| Attrition by Gender | Shows attrition distribution by gender |
| Job Role Summary | Compares attrition count, average age, and average years of service by job role |
| Attrition by Age Group | Highlights attrition across age ranges |
| Attrition by Salary Slab | Compares attrition across salary bands |
| Slicers | Filters the dashboard by department, job role, and education field |

## 💡 Business Questions
1. Which education fields have the highest attrition counts?
2. How does attrition vary across job roles?
3. Which age groups show higher attrition counts?
4. How is attrition distributed across salary slabs?
5. How do the KPIs change when a department or education field is selected?

## 🔍 Key Takeaways
Use the dashboard to explore workforce patterns and identify employee segments that may need further investigation. Add **verified insights from your own report** here—for example, the highest-attrition job role or salary slab—after checking the complete, unfiltered dataset. Filtered views can show different KPI values, so avoid treating a filtered result as an overall company result.
